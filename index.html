03
Field Observations
Botanical insights from the data
Setosa Characteristics
◉

Distinctly compact petals averaging 1.46 cm in length and 0.24 cm in width — far smaller than the other two species, enabling near-perfect linear separation.

◉

Broad sepals despite short length, yielding a high sepal width-to-length ratio — a reliable morphological marker unique to this species.

Versicolor vs. Virginica
◉

Intermediate petal dimensions in Versicolor (avg 4.26 × 1.33 cm) versus the larger Virginica (avg 5.55 × 2.03 cm) — overlap exists but petal area offers strong discrimination.

◉

Virginica's sepal length (avg 6.59 cm) surpasses Versicolor (avg 5.94 cm), though the distributions overlap — making multivariate models essential for accurate classification.
